Methodology and data sources

Historical data
Tesla 2020–2021 daily price and volume history, with a labeled split-adjustment indicator.
Delayed/near-live data
Indicative stock and options data from an approved provider; quotes and trades are delayed, and this is labeled, never presented as live.
Calculations
Premiums, break-evens, maximum loss, and payoff are computed with deterministic, tested logic. AI explains the results; it does not perform the arithmetic.
