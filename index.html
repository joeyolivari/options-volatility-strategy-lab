THE RESEARCH FOUNDATION

Transparent by design.

Understand where the data comes from, how results are calculated, and what the evidence can support.

Historical data
Tesla 2020–2021 daily price and volume history, with a labeled split-adjustment indicator.
Delayed/near-live data
Planned indicative or delayed stock and options feeds. Public display requires provider-terms review; the source, feed delay, and achieved refresh cadence must be labeled.
Calculations
Premiums, break-evens, maximum loss, and payoff are computed with deterministic, tested logic. AI explains the results; it does not perform the arithmetic.
Value classification
Published figures must carry the applicable classifications: Historical, Raw, Split-adjusted, Indicative, Delayed, Illustrative, or Simulated. The word “Live” is never used by itself—delayed and periodically refreshed data is always labeled as such.
Freshness states
Each data-backed section reports one of: Fresh, Stale, Refresh failed, or Insufficient data. Sections currently show “Insufficient data” while datasets and published reports are being prepared.
